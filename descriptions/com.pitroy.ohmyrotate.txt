OhMyRotate - smarter rotation, especially for video.

Take control of portrait and landscape rotation without constantly opening Control Center. Honestly, I think it's smarter than Apple's approach - and most rotation tweaks I've seen in years. :D

- Automatically disable portrait lock for selected apps.
- Unlock rotation during supported video playback.
- Even keep videos locked in landscape while you watch.

Completely free.  
English and Vietnamese support.  
For iPhone, with Rootless and RootHide support.

If you run into any issues, please enable logging in the tweak's settings, reproduce the issue, and send me the log.
