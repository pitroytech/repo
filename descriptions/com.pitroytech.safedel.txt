SafeDel - offload apps from the Home Screen, keep their data.

SafeDel saves you from a small disaster: deleting an app by mistake and losing all of its data. It adds a menu that removes only the app itself and keeps its data (everything you did in the app, sign-ins, settings, cache...).

Nothing fancy here. It simply brings iOS's own Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Offload App to the Home Screen.

- Add an orange Offload App item to the icon menu
- Hide Remove App from the menu
- Make the X button offload the app instead of deleting it
- Show how much space offloading or deleting will free

Simple, but handy. Enjoy!
Tested on iOS 15.0.2 (RootHide) and 17.2.1 (Dopamine). Rootless and RootHide.
