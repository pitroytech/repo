SimpleRightMenu - reorder, hide, and compact the iOS text selection menu.

Put the text actions you use within easy reach. SimpleRightMenu lets you organize the native iOS text selection menu, reduce clutter, choose a compact look, and add useful search tools.

What you can do:

- Your menu, your order: Drag and drop available native actions such as Copy, Translate, Find Selection and Look Up into your preferred order.

- Less clutter: Hide actions you do not use. New Quick Note is hidden by default.

- Three display styles: Choose Text, Icon + Text, or compact Icon Only. Paste and Search has a distinct clipboard-and-magnifier icon so it is easy to tell apart from Paste.

- Search with AI: On supported iOS 16+ edit menus, open a small editable popup with your selected text and an English or Vietnamese prompt. Adjust the request before sending it. Only Send opens Google AI Mode in the browser with the complete edited query, with a Safari fallback. The prompt does not force the response language.

- Search Google / Open Link: Search the web for selected text, or open a selected web address.

- Google Translate: Open the selected text in Google Translate, using your device language as the target language.

- Always Show Select All: Add Select All when part of the text is selected in supported, nonsecure text fields.

- Localized settings: English and Vietnamese settings make it easy to reorder actions, control visibility and toggle extra tools. Preference updates apply immediately, with no respring required.

Available actions depend on the current app, selection and field type. Requires iOS 15 or later. Rootless and RootHide packages are available.
